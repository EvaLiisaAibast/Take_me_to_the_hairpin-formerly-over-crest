HOW TO CALL A NOTE
You are the co-driver. The shorthand is your pace note — say it in plain English before the clock runs out.
R4 100 L2! means "right medium, then in 100 metres, left very tight — caution".
Match the meaning — phrasing is forgiven. Calling early or late both cost you. Press H anytime for shortcuts. This hint shows on your first two stages only.
Translate the note — type what it means (Enter to submit)
